Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 22, 2026

Introduction

Krobo Girls SHS ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

Our app does not collect any personal information. We do not require registration, login, or any form of user account.

Automatic Information

The app uses Google AdMob for advertising, which may automatically collect certain anonymous information for ad personalization, including:

  • Device advertising ID
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General location (not precise GPS)
  • App usage data for ad analytics

Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

How We Use Information

  • To serve relevant advertisements
  • To improve the app experience
  • To monitor app performance and stability

Data Storage

All app data (club listings, programmes, news, notices) is stored locally on your device using Hive. No data is transmitted to external servers by the app itself.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences, including students, parents, and staff of Krobo Girls SHS. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through our app, please contact us.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Reset your advertising ID in your device settings
  • Opt out of ad personalization in Google Ads settings
  • Clear app data from your device settings at any time

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted within the app and on this page.
